About & Engineering Philosophy

First-principles systems engineering, edge computing, and trust-centric intelligence.

Engineering from First Principles

I design and build software systems where performance, determinism, and craftsmanship are non-negotiable. Rather than relying on superficial abstractions or boilerplate templates, I architect software from first principles: whether that means building real-time multi-object tracking pipelines in Python with OpenCV and ByteTrack, compiling edge-AI models into the browser using TensorFlow.js with zero server latency, or rendering 60fps 3D aerial simulations with WebGL and Three.js.

My work spans production AI systems, location-first civic platforms for rural India, and high-performance web applications. I care deeply about minimalist product design, sub-100ms load times, and interfaces that respect human attention.
