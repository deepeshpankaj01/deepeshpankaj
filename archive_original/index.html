HAi Pilot (HealthAI)

AI Oncology Co-Pilot for Doctors in India

Oncologists and physicians face heavy clinical decision pressure and complex multidimensional patient histories. HAi Pilot analyzes structured incoming patient data, performs algorithmic matching against 5 similar historical oncology cases from clinical datasets, and surfaces evidence-based treatment insights in seconds.

▹ Dual-Portal Architecture: Structured patient intake portal on one side; real-time oncology triage dashboard on the other.
▹ Clinical Decision Support: Surfaces treatment protocols, ICD-10 cross-referencing, and similar patient cohort outcomes.
▹ One-Click PDF Export: Generates formatted clinical case reports directly using client-side jsPDF rendering.
▹ Scholarly Foundation: Direct implementation of Deepesh's published paper "HAI: The Trust-Centric Paradigm in Clinical AI".

★ International Conference Presentation · ICAIEST-2026

HAI: The Trust-Centric Paradigm in Clinical AI

Presented at the International Conference on Advanced Innovation in Engineering, Science and Technology (ICAIEST-2026), held at LNCT University, Bhopal (Feb 27–28, 2026).

Abstract & Core Thesis: AI adoption in critical clinical environments (such as oncology) is hindered by black-box skepticism and decision fatigue. This paper proposes a trust-centric paradigm prioritizing explainability, deterministic historical case retrieval, and human-in-the-loop validation over opaque generative inference. This research directly serves as the theoretical architecture behind the HAi Pilot oncology co-pilot application.
